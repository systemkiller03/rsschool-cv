About Me

I am a Computer Science student and aspiring Full-Stack Developer. I have been gradually improving my skills in both frontend and backend development, but recently I have been focusing more on the backend and gaining a deeper understanding of how things work behind the interface. While doing this, I realized that my frontend skills are not developing at the same pace as my backend skills. Therefore, one of my current goals is to strengthen my frontend knowledge and become more balanced as a full-stack developer. I want to improve not only in building interfaces, but also in understanding frontend architecture, state management, performance, and good development practices. One of my strengths is my analytical approach to learning and problem-solving. I tend to question how and why something works, look for the reasoning behind different approaches, and think about the possible consequences before choosing a solution. I enjoy going beyond simply making something work and trying to understand whether it is the right way to solve the problem. I am curious and enjoy learning how things work beneath the surface. I see each new technology as an opportunity to understand something more deeply and identify areas where I can continue improving.
